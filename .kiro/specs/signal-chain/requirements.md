# Requirements: signal chain (The Wire)

## Introduction
> After the workspace move (spec `skeleton`), the modem and transports live in `apps/dead-air`; the seeded channel and the engine live in the kit.

Two devices exchange short messages for the drill. The link is a resurrected acoustic FSK modem (speaker to microphone) with a seeded simulated channel as the equal-behaviour fallback. Design and tasks are to be generated by Kiro from this file; include correctness properties for every requirement marked (PBT).

## Glossary
- **Frame**: preamble + sync word + length + payload (≤ 64 bytes) + CRC-16.
- **Transport**: anything that implements `send(frame)` and emits `received | garbled | collision` events.
- **Channel profile**: delay range, drop probability, bit-flip probability, scripted by the scenario per time window.

## Requirements

### 1. Framing
1. WHEN a payload of 0–64 bytes is framed and then parsed, THE Modem SHALL return the identical payload. (PBT)
2. WHEN any single bit of a frame is flipped, THE Modem SHALL report the frame as garbled and SHALL NOT deliver the payload as valid. (PBT)
3. IF a payload exceeds 64 bytes, THEN THE Modem SHALL reject it before transmission with a typed error.

### 2. Modulation
1. WHEN a frame is modulated to samples and demodulated with no added noise, THE Modem SHALL recover the identical frame at 44.1 kHz and 48 kHz. (PBT)
2. WHEN white noise is added at 12 dB SNR or better, THE Modem SHALL recover at least 95% of frames in a 200-frame seeded trial. Record the measured rate in `docs/RESULTS.md`.
3. WHILE a device is transmitting, THE Modem SHALL NOT decode incoming audio.
4. WHEN a modulated frame is preceded and followed by 0–2 s of silence or low-level noise, THE Modem SHALL find and recover the frame. (PBT)
5. WHEN the modulated signal is scaled by any gain from 0.05 to 1.0, or carries a DC offset up to 0.1, THE Modem SHALL recover the identical frame. (PBT)
6. WHEN samples are fed to the demodulator in chunks of any size from 1 to 4096 samples, THE Modem SHALL emit the same frames as when given the whole buffer at once. (PBT) The AudioWorklet delivers 128-sample blocks, so this is the real operating mode.
7. WHEN two frames are sent back to back with a gap of 50 ms or more, THE Modem SHALL recover both, in order. (PBT)
8. WHEN one echo of the signal is added at 5–40 ms delay and −6 dB or lower, THE Modem SHALL recover at least 95% of frames in a 200-frame seeded trial. Record the result; if it fails, lower the baud rate and record that too.
9. WHEN the sender and receiver sample clocks differ by up to 0.1%, THE Modem SHALL recover a 64-byte frame.
10. THE repository SHALL contain at least one real recording (laptop speaker to phone or laptop microphone at 1 m, WAV, made by the developer) as a test fixture, and a test that decodes it.

### 3. Simulated channel
1. WHEN two runs use the same seed, channel profile and send times, THE Channel SHALL produce the identical sequence of deliveries, drops, garbles and collisions. (PBT)
2. WHEN two frames overlap in time on the channel, THE Channel SHALL emit a collision to both parties and deliver neither.
3. THE Channel SHALL never deliver a frame earlier than its send time plus the profile's minimum delay. (PBT)

### 4. Transports
1. THE System SHALL provide three transports behind one interface: acoustic, loopback (two tabs via BroadcastChannel), and scripted (solo partner).
2. WHEN microphone permission is denied or unavailable, THE System SHALL fall back to loopback or scripted and say so in text.
3. WHEN the acoustic transport receives a frame, THE System SHALL report the measured airtime and signal level to the engine as event metadata.

### 5. Engine integration
1. WHEN a transport emits `received`, `garbled` or `collision`, THE Engine SHALL append exactly one event with sim-time, sender, recipient and outcome to the log.
2. WHEN a log is replayed, THE Engine SHALL reach the same state without touching any transport. (PBT)
