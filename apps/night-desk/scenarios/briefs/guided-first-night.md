# Guided first night
## Participants
- ra: Jo Marlowe - the resident assistant on duty, at the desk in the lounge of the fictional Linden Hall
- phone: Duty phone - the phone you carry on duty
- chat: Floor chat - the group chat for the second floor
- door: The door - whoever knocks at the lounge
- staff: Ms Alder - the staff member on call, a phone call away
## Beats
- 0 | ra | message_created | Text on the duty phone | Priya in 204 writes: The music in 206 is loud again. Could someone come and ask them to turn it down?
- 3 | ra | message_created | Floor chat | Someone in the floor chat writes: Is that bass from 206 or is it just me? Anyone else hearing it?
- 11 | ra | message_created | A knock at the door | A student from the next floor knocks and asks if the noise is being dealt with.
- 20 | all | debrief | Night ends | The hall settles. The replay shows what had reached you at each choice.
## Decisions
### ra: Priya has texted about the music. What do you do?
- read-text: Read the text and reply that you are on your way
- reply-chat: Answer the floor chat
- answer-door: Open the door to the student who knocked
- call-staff: Phone Ms Alder, the staff member on call
- wait: Wait a couple of minutes
