Hindsight

Drills where information arrives late, damaged or never. Afterwards, a replay shows what each person knew at the moment they decided.

Night Desk

A solo rehearsal of a resident assistant's duty night, for one person on one laptop. Calls, knocks and messages arrive while you are busy with something else, and doing anything costs minutes.

In progress. There is no playable night yet.

Dead Air

A drill for two people whose only link is sound. One device plays a message as tones, the other hears it through its microphone. If both talk at once, both messages are lost.

In progress. The page currently holds an early prototype of the drill.

Every scenario here is invented. This is practice, and it does not replace your organisation's own training.
